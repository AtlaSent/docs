## 1. Start with one protected action

Choose one consequential action with a clear execution boundary. Define the actor, target/resource, environment, approval inputs, and decision-material fields that must not change after authorization.

## 2. Confirm the organization boundary

Use the authoritative AtlaSent organization/tenant. The selected organization determines customer scoping; it does not itself grant authority for the protected action.

## 3. Connect the minimum evidence

Connect or inspect the systems needed for the first action. Product state must be explicit: **connected**, **setup required**, or **unavailable**. Do not treat a placeholder integration as connected.

## 4. Run a rehearsal

Use the supported rehearsal/shadow path. A rehearsal may make a real evaluation call, but it must not fabricate a Permit or consequential downstream effect.

Interpret outcomes separately:

- `allow` — authorization decision only
- `deny` — request refused
- `hold` — additional required input/approval is unresolved
- `escalate` — route to the applicable escalation process

## 5. Review evidence

Confirm the decision, authority/approval inputs, policy/version references, and Permit/verification evidence that actually exists. Do not infer execution from an earlier lifecycle stage.

## 6. Check readiness before production

Production activation is separate from signup and rehearsal. Before activation confirm:

- you are working in the correct organization
- your primary user can sign in
- your plan includes the capabilities you intend to use
- each integration you rely on shows as connected, not setup required
- you know how to reach support
- any setup step that AtlaSent performs on your behalf is listed in your implementation plan
