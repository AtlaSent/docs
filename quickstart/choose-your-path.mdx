## One lifecycle

All three paths use the same lifecycle:

**Verified identity → organizational authority → authorization → Permit → verification → execution → evidence**

Do not collapse the stages. An `allow` does not prove execution. Permit verification does not prove the downstream effect occurred.

<Note>
Quickstarts default to sandbox or rehearsal. Production activation remains a separate, controlled acceptance step.
</Note>
