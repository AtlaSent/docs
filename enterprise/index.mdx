**AtlaSent Enterprise** (custom annual pricing) is the operating layer for Consequential Action Protection across teams, systems and business units: the Protection Portfolio, honest coverage of every execution path, identity and administration, organizational controls such as separation of duties, findings, evidence and proof, and implementation and support. It is scoped through an Enterprise engagement, starting with Enterprise Launch (assisted implementation of your first protected action), and it is separate from the self-service Community, Developer Cloud and Team Cloud plans. [Schedule an Enterprise call](https://cal.com/atlasent/discovery).

The pages in this section document specific Enterprise capabilities for organizations with strict identity governance, data-residency, and audit-evidence requirements.

<Warning>
**Availability, as of 2026-09-27.** This page documents Enterprise capabilities; it is not a list of what every Enterprise agreement includes. "Deployed; customer validation pending" means the capability runs in production but has not yet been validated with a customer's own systems (for SSO and SCIM, a customer identity provider). That is different from unavailable. Org-wide login-time SSO enforcement is not offered. Self-hosted Helm deployment is not yet released. What your agreement includes, and on what timeline, is confirmed in writing before you sign. Contact [contact@atlasent.io](mailto:contact@atlasent.io).
</Warning>
