## What AtlaSent is, in one paragraph

AtlaSent is **execution-time authorization for AI agents in GxP environments**. When an AI agent in your stack wants to take a consequential action — write to an EHR, release a batch, modify a trial record — it calls AtlaSent first. AtlaSent checks the action against a RACI policy your Quality team authored, returns one of the canonical four-value decisions (`allow` / `deny` / `hold` / `escalate`) in milliseconds, and writes a tamper-evident audit entry. Every decision is signed; the audit chain is exportable as evidence for FDA or EMA inspection.

## What it isn't

- **Not a medical device.** AtlaSent is a SaaS platform, not a medical device. Using it with medical-device software requires your own separate assessment.
- **Not an AI model.** We do not train or fine-tune. AtlaSent has no access to patient data unless you explicitly log a target ID — and we strongly recommend you do not.
- **Not a replacement for your QMS.** AtlaSent authorizes agent actions; your QMS still governs your SOPs, CAPAs, and deviation handling. AtlaSent feeds signals into both.
