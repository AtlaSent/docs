AtlaSent reasons about authorization with a small set of **concepts** — the model of *authority* (who may act, on what evidence, producing what proof). The [Protected Action Canon](/reference/protected-action-canon) describes *what is governed*. This page is where the two meet: for each concept, the canonical actions that realize it.

The Atlas defines **17 concepts**; **16** are realized by at least one of the **56** protected actions. The mapping is **deterministically derived** from each action's authorization requirements — it is not a hand-authored opinion.

> **Absence from this map does not mean an action is unsupported at runtime.** A concept with no linked actions is simply one no *canonical* action realizes yet (for example, an emerging concept). Runtime support is governed by policy and configuration, not by this reference.
