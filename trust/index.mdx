## What AtlaSent is

AtlaSent is execution-time authorization infrastructure. It sits in the
execution path of high-impact automated actions — AI agents, CI/CD
pipelines, regulated workflows — and answers one question before any
consequential action runs: *is this actor authorized to take this action
right now, against this target, in this context?*

We operate AtlaSent as a multi-tenant SaaS service. Customer data is
logically segregated by `organization_id` at the database row-level
security layer. The canonical decision evidence is an immutable,
append-only, hash-chained audit log (GA today). **Portable Ed25519-signed
evidence bundles** assembled from that log for offline third-party
verification are on the published roadmap; see
[audit and evidence posture](#audit-and-evidence-posture) below for the
explicit boundary between the two trust surfaces.

AtlaSent does not, and has not in the preceding 12 months, sold or
shared (as those terms are defined in the CCPA) Personal Information.
Customer Personal Data submitted to the Service is governed by the
DPA; AtlaSent does not use Customer Personal Data to train any
general-purpose machine-learning or large-language model. See the
Privacy Policy for details.

See the compliance claims matrix for the canonical
list of what AtlaSent is and isn't aligned with today.

## Audit and evidence posture

Two distinct trust surfaces. Procurement and Quality reviewers should not
collapse them; the distinction is architectural and material.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Immutable audit chain — GA today">
    Hash-chained, append-only ledger of every authorization decision and
    every Console mutation. Permit tokens are bound into each entry by
    cryptographic reference. The chain is internal to AtlaSent and
    queryable in-place via the API. It provides event continuity,
    append-only integrity, and runtime evidence lineage. Customer
    verification today is API-mediated inspection of the chain.
    **Available today.** Cryptographic primitives are documented in
    DPA Annex II.1 (AtlaSent's contractual representation).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portable signed evidence bundles — roadmap">
    Detached, deterministic, Ed25519-signed packages assembled from the
    audit chain for portable offline verification by third-party
    auditors. Specification source is maintained in
    `architecture/evidence-bundles-spec.md`. **This portal does not
    represent portable signed evidence bundles as GA until the
    corresponding customer-facing release and verification surface is
    published and the trust documentation is updated. They are not
    currently part of the Service committed under the MSA, DPA, or SLA,
    and AtlaSent does not warrant availability on a particular date.**
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The distinction will be preserved on this portal if and when the portable
bundle surface reaches GA.

## Vendor qualification chain

The parent VQP is the authoritative baseline; material changes between
annual refreshes are issued as Delta VQPs bound to the parent commit
(ADR-013, Accepted 2026-05-18).
A customer's current qualification is the parent VQP plus the applicable
Delta chain.

- Parent VQP: 2026-04-19 (V1 docs gate)
- Delta chain: available on request from `compliance@atlasent.io`
- Latest Delta recorded on this portal: `2026-05-20.01` — trust subdomain cutover (announces canonical URL `trust.atlasent.io`; URL surface change only, no capability change). Prior Delta: `2026-05-18.02` (signatory completion supersession-in-part of `2026-05-18.01`).
- **Operational target.** Deltas are targeted for issuance within five
  business days of a trigger event. Until the automated Delta VQP
  issuance pipeline is operational, this is an internal operational
  target rather than a contractual commitment unless expressly
  incorporated into an executed agreement.

Until the Delta VQP issuance pipeline is operational, Deltas are issued
as commits to `atlasent-docs/vqp/deltas/` and customer verification
proceeds via commit SHA inspection against this repository. The portal
does not represent the planned automated manifest, feed, or Console
surfaces as currently available.

## What buyers usually want first

Based on procurement conversations:

1. **CAIQ.** Pre-fill is maintained in source control and updated
   quarterly. Email `compliance@atlasent.io` for the latest version.
2. **SOC 2 report.** Type II is on roadmap for Q4 2026 (see
   claims matrix). Type I report will be made
   available on this portal upon completion of the audit period; the
   Type II report will be available under NDA.
3. **DPA.** Available on request from `legal@atlasent.io`. EU SCCs Module 2/3 and UK
   IDTA are incorporated.
4. **BAA.** Currently "Not yet available — contact sales" per the
   claims matrix. The BAA status will not flip
   to "Available" until AtlaSent has designated a HIPAA Privacy
   Officer and Security Officer and operationally tested its
   breach-notification workflow, in line with 45 C.F.R. §§
   164.308(a)(2) and 164.530(a)(1)(i). Pilot customers requiring PHI
   should reach `compliance@atlasent.io`.
5. **Penetration test summary.** Most recent third-party report
   available under NDA on request to `security@atlasent.io`. The NDA
   carries the DTSA §1833(b) immunity notice required by 18 U.S.C.
   §1833(b)(3).
6. **Sub-processor list.** Public, with 30-day change notification
   for additions/replacements and 60-day notification for material
   changes in scope or location. See [Sub-processors](/trust/subprocessors).

  a corresponding update to DPA Annex II.
