## Urgent security issues

For active breaches, key compromise, or any incident where delay could cause material harm, email [security@atlasent.io](mailto:security@atlasent.io) with the subject line `[URGENT SECURITY]`. Include a brief description of the situation, the systems involved, and the best way to reach you immediately.

<Warning>
  **[URGENT SECURITY] is for active incidents only.** Vulnerability reports that are not under active exploitation should use the standard `[Security]` subject line. Using the urgent path for non-urgent reports delays response to real incidents.
</Warning>

## What to include with your inquiry

Including the following information upfront shortens the round trip for procurement and security reviews:

- Your **organization name** and your **role** (e.g., CISO, DPO, Quality Director, Procurement).
- Whether an **NDA** is in place, and if so, with whom and dated when.
- A description of your **use case** and the **categories of regulated data** you plan to process.
- Your **timeline** and any **procurement deadline**.

For privacy and data subject requests, include the identifiers Atlasent would use to locate the data — typically the account email address. If you are an end user of an Atlasent customer (rather than the customer itself), direct your request to that customer — Atlasent will assist the customer in responding.

## What not to send by email

- **Customer Data, PHI, or regulated content** — use the Atlasent product for that. If you need to share an example for diagnostic purposes, scrub or synthesize it first.
- **Credentials or API keys** — rotate the key immediately and tell Atlasent the key ID, not the value.
