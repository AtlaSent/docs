## What a sub-processor is

A sub-processor is a third party AtlaSent engages to process Customer
Personal Data in connection with the AtlaSent service. The categories of
Customer Personal Data, processing location, and cross-border transfer
mechanism for each sub-processor are documented at the canonical link
above.

## Change notification

We notify customers of any **addition** or **replacement** of a
sub-processor processing Customer Personal Data **at least 30 days**
before the change takes effect.

Customers who object to a new sub-processor in good faith may terminate
the affected service within the notice window without penalty for the
unused portion of any pre-paid fees, per DPA §11.

## Subscribe to change notifications

- **Email** `subprocessors@atlasent.io` with subject `subscribe` and the
