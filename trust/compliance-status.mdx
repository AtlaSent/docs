This page is the buyer-friendly summary of Atlasent's compliance posture. It answers the question "are you [framework] compliant?" for every framework that comes up in enterprise procurement. Atlasent does not claim attestations or certifications it does not hold, and every "on roadmap" entry carries a dated target so you can plan accordingly.

<Note>
  Atlasent is transparent about gaps. If a framework you need is not listed as "Active" or "Supported," assume it is not available today and contact [compliance@atlasent.io](mailto:compliance@atlasent.io) before making procurement decisions that depend on it.
</Note>

## What "customer-supporting" means

A customer-supporting status means Atlasent provides the documentation, primitives, and evidence artifacts you need to satisfy the framework's requirements in your own regulated environment — but Atlasent itself does not hold a certification or attestation under that framework. For GxP frameworks, this means you get framework mapping tables, policy presets, and a Vendor Qualification Package (VQP) that your Quality team uses to qualify Atlasent as a supplier.

<Tip>
  "Customer-supporting" for GxP is the standard posture for software tools used within regulated processes. Your organization remains the regulated entity; Atlasent is a qualified supplier. The VQP provides the documented evidence your Quality team needs to complete that qualification.
</Tip>

## HIPAA BAA status

The HIPAA BAA is not yet available for general availability. Atlasent is not currently configured to support protected health information (PHI). If you require a BAA, email [compliance@atlasent.io](mailto:compliance@atlasent.io) to be added to the waitlist. For evaluation and testing, use synthetic or de-identified data only — the HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification standard at 45 CFR §164.514(b)(2) is a practical baseline.

<Warning>
  Do not submit PHI to Atlasent until a signed BAA is in place. Atlasent will not accept a BAA engagement unless the operational prerequisites — designated Privacy and Security Officers and tested breach-notification workflow — are in place.
</Warning>

## GDPR and data protection

Atlasent acts as a **processor** for personal data your agents submit through the service and as a **controller** for account-holder data collected directly. The DPA incorporates EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Modules 2 and 3) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Request the current DPA template from [privacy@atlasent.io](mailto:privacy@atlasent.io) — turnaround is 2 business days.

Atlasent does not sell or share personal information as defined under CCPA. Customer Personal Data submitted to the service is not used to train any general-purpose machine learning or large language model.

## Vendor Qualification Package (VQP)

The Vendor Qualification Package is a structured document designed for regulated-industry customers. It maps Atlasent's controls to GxP framework requirements so your Quality team can complete supplier qualification without building the mapping from scratch.

The VQP contains the following ten sections:

<Accordion title="VQP table of contents">
  1. **Vendor profile** — company overview, contact information, regulatory experience
  2. **System description** — architecture overview, data flows, deployment model
  3. **Control mapping** — 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, ICH E6 GCP requirement-to-primitive tables
  4. **Change control** — how Atlasent manages changes to the service and notifies customers
  5. **Incident response** — documented IR plan summary, notification commitments
  6. **Business continuity** — RPO, RTO, backup and restore procedures
  7. **Sub-processors** — full sub-processor list with data categories and locations
  8. **Security controls** — encryption, access controls, vulnerability management
  9. **Audit and evidence** — audit chain design, export procedures, offline verification
  10. **Contractual schedules** — DPA Annex II technical and organizational measures, SLA commitments
</Accordion>

To request the VQP, email [compliance@atlasent.io](mailto:compliance@atlasent.io) with your organization name, the framework(s) you are qualifying against, and your timeline. Atlasent responds within 5 business days.
