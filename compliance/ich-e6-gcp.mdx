ICH E6 is the international **Good Clinical Practice** guideline for clinical trials. It applies to AI agents handling protocol data, subject records, adverse events, and trial master files.

## Requirement-to-primitive mapping

Section numbers below follow **ICH E6(R2)**. ICH E6(R3) renumbers these sections; map them to the current revision in your own validation documentation.

| E6(R2) § | Requirement | AtlaSent primitive |
|---|---|---|
| 2.10  | Source data must be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate | [Permit tokens](/concepts/permit) attribute each AI action to a specific agent + timestamp; [ALCOA+ mapping](/compliance/alcoa-plus). |
| 5.5.3 | Sponsor oversight of electronic systems | Policies are authored by the sponsor; activations are audit-logged. |
| 5.5.4 | Access / audit trails | Immutable [audit chain](/concepts/audit-chain) on every evaluation. |
| 6.4.9 | Data collection — authorized personnel only | RACI rules restrict which agents can collect / modify which data classes. |
| 8.3   | Essential documents — evidence of actions | Permit IDs are the evidence; stored with the record in your eCRF. |
