Atlasent is purpose-built for regulated industries where every authorization decision must be traceable, immutable, and auditable. Rather than requiring you to bolt compliance onto a general-purpose authorization tool, Atlasent ships with framework-specific policy presets, an append-only hash-linked audit chain, and verifiable permit tokens that satisfy the evidence requirements of FDA, EMA, and ICH regulations out of the box. Every claim in this section points to a concrete product primitive — no hand-waving, no aspirational language.

## How Atlasent handles compliance

Atlasent enforces authorization at execution time, which means consequential actions — writing a record, releasing a batch, deploying to production — cannot run without a valid, unexpired permit that is bound into the audit chain before the API responds. This architectural choice makes compliance evidence a natural by-product of normal system operation rather than something you reconstruct after the fact.

<Tip>
  Request the **Vendor Qualification Package (VQP)** if your Quality team needs a structured mapping of Atlasent controls to GxP framework requirements. Email [compliance@atlasent.io](mailto:compliance@atlasent.io) to request a copy.
</Tip>

## What Atlasent deliberately does not claim

Atlasent is transparent about gaps. The following are not available today:

- **SOC 2 Type II** — audit observation window is on the roadmap for Q4 2026.
- **HIPAA BAA** — not yet available for general availability; contact sales for pilot arrangements.
- **ISO 27001 certification** — not in V1 scope; controls are mapped in the GxP Change Control crosswalk.
- **FDA 510(k) / medical device** — Atlasent is not a medical device; use with medical-device software requires a separate IEC 62304 assessment.

If you need a claim that is not listed here, ask before assuming. Email [compliance@atlasent.io](mailto:compliance@atlasent.io) with the framework and section — Atlasent will respond in writing.
