EU Annex 11 is the **EMA guideline for computerized systems** in regulated GMP environments. It applies to AI agents processing GMP data, clinical trial data, and regulated manufacturing records.

## Requirement-to-primitive mapping

| Clause | Requirement | AtlaSent primitive |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1  | Data integrity | [Audit chain](/concepts/audit-chain) is append-only + hash-linked; permits bind records to authorizations. |
| 9    | Audit trails | Every `/evaluate` call produces a timestamped, attributed chain entry. |
| 12.1 | Security — physical / logical controls restricting access | Scoped [API keys](/api-reference/authentication#scopes); [policy](/concepts/policy) restricts agent action classes; policy-based authorization + fail-closed API. |
| 13   | Incident management | Deny events surface in the Console deviation queue. |
| 14, 15 | Electronic signature; batch release | Ed25519-signed [permit tokens](/concepts/permit) as the authorization record. |
| 17   | Archiving | [Signed audit bundle export](/quickstart/audit-export) — verifiable long after retrieval. |
